# IANA time zone data.
# zoneinfo/ZoneInfo reads it from the OS, and a slim container image often
# ships no tzdata at all -- so `ZoneInfo("Asia/Kolkata")` raises at import time
# and takes the whole app down. Pinning it as a dependency is the fix that does
# not depend on the base image.
